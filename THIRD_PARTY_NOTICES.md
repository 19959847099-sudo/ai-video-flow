The exact shipped FFmpeg and FFprobe executables have SHA-256 `a35880a72ca511bbf595a28c6480c85218cdf1d6c1008511a848fc71dbad5527` and `493c63c393a509ab210751252fa70270330ad7413c538dce78b29adddf7ffc85`. Their reported configuration contains `--enable-gpl --enable-version3` and `libx264`/`libx265`, so this is **not** an LGPL-only binary. Corresponding source is delivered from a generated manifest: [`third_party/ffmpeg/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`](third_party/ffmpeg/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json) resolves the vendor's pinned build recipe against the configuration the shipped binary reports and lists every upstream source repository at an exact revision, and [`tools/ffmpeg_provenance.py`](tools/ffmpeg_provenance.py) fetches and hash-verifies those archives. [`third_party/ffmpeg/PROVENANCE.md`](third_party/ffmpeg/PROVENANCE.md) records the chain, the verification commands and the two Subversion-pinned components (`libmp3lame`, `xvid`) that are delivered by exact-revision retrieval instruction rather than as mirrored archives.

Qt Virtual Keyboard is GPL-only under the [Qt module list](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licensing.html); it is unused and explicitly excluded from the Windows bundle. Qt for Python's LGPL conditions and third-party licenses within the remaining Qt modules need a final distributable-contents check after every packaging change. This file documents provenance and known obligations; it is not a legal opinion.
